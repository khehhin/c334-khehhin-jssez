The SETUP framework

Five steps to get organised

The framework connects location, incentives, legal setup, bilateral support and talent.

  1. S

    Select your zone strategically

    Compare the designated zones and align their activities with your business model and operational needs.

  2. E

    Evaluate incentives

    Check MIDA incentive packages, Enterprise Singapore support and Invest Malaysia Facilitation Centre guidance.

  3. T

    Take the right legal steps

    Choose an appropriate structure, register the business, apply for needed licences and register for tax and employee requirements.

  4. U

    Use bilateral support

    Work with the JS-SEZ Project Office, IMFC-J, SMF and the Federation of Malaysian Manufacturers for facilitation.

  5. P

    Prepare talent and supply chains

    Plan workforce mobility, upskilling and supply-chain readiness as part of the operating model.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pages 6-7.

Tax watch-outs

  • Incentives are sector- and zone-specific.
  • Different requirements apply in Singapore and Malaysia.
  • Related-party transactions need fair pricing and proper records under transfer-pricing rules.
  • Build the operating model first, then select incentives that fit it.

People and mobility

Passes for staff and investors

Employment Pass

MIDA considers expatriate positions for the Employment Pass by category. Approval depends on the availability of Malaysian expertise and the project's criteria.

Playbook page 28

Professional Visit Pass

This temporary pass can support foreign professionals providing training, consultancy or project-based work on behalf of an overseas company.

Playbook page 28

Investor Pass

The Investor Pass supports eligible business travellers across new investors, investors in the pipeline and existing investors.
